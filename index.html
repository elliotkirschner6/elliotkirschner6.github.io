About

I’ve never been especially interested in staying in one lane.

I studied American history and literature, then went into journalism. Those instincts still shape nearly everything I do: look backward for context, look outward for evidence, and look closely at people rather than reducing them to abstractions.

Over the years that has led me from network newsrooms and reporting trips to books, newsletters, documentary features, science films, classrooms, and advisory work with people and organizations trying to explain complicated ideas clearly.

I am especially drawn to subjects that connect disciplines: politics with history, science with culture, art with public life, and individual experience with the larger forces shaping our world.
